Large media use the ordinary Bulk continuation marker in byte 4:
0x00 on the first logical packet, 0x02 on continuation packets.
The complete-object size/CRC, timeSign and imageIndex remain stable.

Direct original-hardware observation: items advance at approximately 3 seconds.
The app exposes no Preset dwell control. Since timeSign is consistently 5,
The implementation preserves it as opaque metadata. GIF/image items use the observed ~3000 ms dwell; TEXT timing follows the renderer mode rather than a fixed Preset dwell.

Hardware validation:
- mixed TEXT "PJ" -> GIF -> scrolling TEXT played in slots 14,15,16;
- static/PIN-style TEXT used a ~3000 ms final hold;
- scrolling TEXT advanced after the final glyph left the display (example holdMs=14154 for 17 glyphs at 16x32);
- five GIF/image items in slots 14..18 rotated in command order at ~3000 ms each;
- 48,456-byte and 66,361-byte multi-packet objects committed successfully;
- selecting a new Preset while another was active cleanly replaced the volatile bank.
