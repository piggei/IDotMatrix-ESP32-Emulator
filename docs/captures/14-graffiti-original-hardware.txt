iDotMatrix original hardware - 64x64 Graffiti full-raster capture
Source: Android Bluetooth HCI snoop log
Purpose: protocol reconstruction only; image payload omitted.

Observed logical exchange:

TX packet 1:
  length: 4105
  header: 09 10 00 00 00 00 30 00 00
  marker: 00
  total raster bytes: 12288
  RGB payload bytes: 4096
RX notification:
  05 00 00 00 02

TX packet 2:
  length: 4105
  header prefix: 09 10 00 00 02 00 30 00 00
  marker: 02
  total raster bytes: 12288
  RGB payload bytes: 4096
RX notification:
  05 00 00 00 02

TX packet 3:
  length: 4105
  header prefix: 09 10 00 00 02 00 30 00 00
  marker: 02
  total raster bytes: 12288
  RGB payload bytes: 4096
RX notification:
  05 00 00 00 01

Confirmed interpretation:
- Graffiti full-raster transport uses a dedicated 9-byte header.
- Type is 0x00.
- Marker 0x00 starts the transfer; marker 0x02 continues it.
- Bytes 5..8 carry total raster size LE.
- RGB payload starts at byte 9.
- 64x64 RGB is 12288 bytes and is transferred as three 4096-byte chunks.
- Status 0x02 is returned while more data is required.
- Status 0x01 is returned after the complete raster has been accepted.
- No CRC32 field is present in this header.
